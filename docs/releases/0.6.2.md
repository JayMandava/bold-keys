# Bold Keys 0.6.2

- Target Android 16 (API 36) for Google Play submissions; retain Android 13 minimum and arm64 support.
- Add a Privacy policy button in Settings > About, opening the published policy in the browser.
- Respect editor no-personalized-learning requests and sensitive fields for learned words and emoji personalization.
- Clarify provider/network descriptions and include accessible offline licenses/credits.
- Expose individual keyboard/suggestion accessibility controls with focus, modifier state and named hold/cursor actions; prevent extra editor/candidate announcements and clear prior-editor candidates.
- Correct privacy wording: password/email fields suppress correction/suggestions; web-address fields may suggest without automatic rewrite.
- Replace unresolved next-word corpora with pinned Common Voice CC0 text alone; expand bounded context coverage without spelling-dictionary ranks or historical chat seeds. Preserve dictionaries byte-for-byte and document held-out results, chat/rare-phrase limits and memory/size costs before shipping.
- Prepare a protected, nonpublishing AAB signing workflow; actual signing and final quality/source-term decisions remain pending.

Production features follow public v0.6.1. No private colour themes, purchases, billing or voice typing are included.
Release signing remains fail-closed. An explicitly unsigned review bundle is not upload-ready and must be signed with the existing production identity before distribution.

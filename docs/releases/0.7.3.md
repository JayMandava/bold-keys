# Bold Keys 0.7.3 — Ocean Drive

- Choose Ocean Drive under Appearance for a neon Vice City tribute with plum keycaps, pink trim, and an aqua trackpad ring.
- Tap the flamingo key to open the symbols picker. Its accessibility label is still “Symbols”.
- Choose Neon Aqua, Neon Pink, or Neon Violet for Ocean Drive. The theme remembers its backlight colour separately from the other palettes. Off, On, Auto, and brightness still work as before.
- Voice typing lights the spacebar in sunset coral while recording and transcribing on Ocean Drive.
- Appearance shows the full keyboard and trackpad inside a rounded enclosure on every theme.

## Fixes
- Double-tap space now inserts a period after emoji, including skin tones, flags, and joined emoji.
- CLOSE is on the left and MORE on the right in the symbols and emoji pickers.
- Hold Shift to lock capital letters; tap Shift to unlock. Normal Shift and double-tap Shift still work.

## Verification
26 focused regression tests, release build and lint, independent review, and physical Android checks covered emoji punctuation, picker layout/paging, and Shift lock/unlock/normal/double-tap behavior. Genuine multi-finger selection and held-Shift shortcuts have automated coverage only.

Voice typing remains deferred. This release does not resume F-Droid publication.

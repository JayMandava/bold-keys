# Bold Keys 0.7.0 — Heritage colours

Three new keyboard colours, with the frame, row trim, trackpad ring, keycaps and readable legends designed together:

- **Red:** black keys and crimson metalwork, inspired by the BlackBerry KEY2 Red Edition.
- **Black & Gold:** dark keys, warm white legends and champagne-gold trim, inspired by the limited black-and-gold BlackBerry Passport.
- **White & Gold:** ivory keys, charcoal legends and gold trim, inspired by the white-and-gold BlackBerry Q10 Special Edition.

Choose a colour in **Settings → Appearance → Key colour**. The preview updates immediately. Black, White and Match phone remain available; existing selections and the default Black appearance are unchanged. Backlight remains a separate choice and works with the dark-key palettes.

The colour picker uses labelled, wrapping choices rather than relying on colour alone. Typing, Shift, Alt, delete, symbols, emoji, trackpad, layouts and privacy behaviour are unchanged.

These are independent design tributes, not official device colour specifications. BlackBerry is a trademark of its owner; Bold Keys is not affiliated with or endorsed by BlackBerry. [Design references](https://github.com/JayMandava/bold-keys/blob/v0.7.0/docs/COLOUR_REFERENCES.md).

Android 13 or newer; arm64. Install as an update to retain your preferences and dictionary.

WORD FREQUENCIES
English, French and German word-frequency tables are derived from Hermit Dave's
FrequencyWords project and OpenSubtitles corpora. Upstream content is licensed
under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International; its code is MIT.
These are separate grants. The app's Apache license does not replace data terms.
Source: https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
Content license: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Bold Keys filters word forms and converts data to tab-separated word/count tables.
French/German processing also filters accent/umlaut variants; scripts are in the
source distribution (tools/build_fr.py and tools/build_de.py). Upstream content
and adapted data remain subject to applicable attribution/share-alike terms.

NEXT-WORD DATA
French/German next-word counts are derived from sentences contributed to Tatoeba,
whose default sentence license is Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France.
Bold Keys tokenizes sentences, counts word pairs, and retains ranked followers.
Source: https://tatoeba.org/
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/
English next-word hints are derived from Peter Norvig's count_2w corpus data.
Source: https://www.norvig.com/ngrams/
No claim is made here that the code license on that page licenses its corpus data.
Dataset snapshot and contributor-attribution review is recorded in the source
repository's docs/THIRD_PARTY_REVIEW.md. These notices do not grant missing rights.
